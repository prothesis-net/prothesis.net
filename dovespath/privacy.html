Dove’s Path Privacy Policy

Effective EFFECTIVE_DATE. Dove’s Path is made by Prothesis LLC (“Prothesis,” “we”).

The short version

  • What you write and say in Dove’s Path is kept on your phone. When you speak a note, Apple may turn your voice into text for the app. Prothesis receives your words only if you choose to send them to us.
  • Prothesis receives three things: your sign-up details, a record of how the app is used, and the Bible passages you respond to.
  • There is no advertising. Nothing is sold, and nothing is sent to build a profile of you for money.
  • You can export your records and delete your account at any time.

What stays on your phone

Everything you write or speak in the app: your notes, prayers, reflections and marks. It is kept in the app’s storage on your phone. No audio of your voice is kept; the app keeps the words as text.

When you speak a note, the app uses Apple’s speech recognition to turn your voice into text. Apple may process that audio on its own servers to do so, under Apple’s privacy policy. Prothesis never receives the audio.

Because these records live on your phone, they can be lost if you lose or change the phone, or delete the app. See “Keeping a copy” below.

What Prothesis receives

Your sign-up details. Your email address, the name you give, and your password, which is stored in a form we cannot read. We use these to sign you in and to reach you about your account.

How the app is used. Which part of the app you are in, how long, which buttons you tap, and when something breaks. Button names are fixed labels written by us; nothing you type is included. This record is not tied to your account, so we cannot tell whose it is.

The Bible passages you respond to. When you write down, save or memorize a verse, we receive the reference and the date and time. We do not receive where you were in the app, and we never receive your own words. The app finds the reference on your phone and sends only that. This record is kept with your account.

What you choose to send us. If you send a problem report, we receive what it contains. If you send a note with the app’s feedback button, write to us by email, or send a screenshot, we receive what you sent. Those are the only ways your own words reach us.

How we use it

  • Sign-up details: to sign you in and to answer you.
  • How the app is used: we look at this as totals across everyone, to find where the app gets in the way and fix it. Because it is not tied to you, there is no record of one person’s use for us to look at. If you need help with a problem, you can choose to send us a report from the app.
  • Bible passages: Scripture is the one set of words we all share, and this lets us notice when people are drawn to the same passage. In time, we hope it can help people who are led to the same Scripture find one another. We will not connect you with anyone unless you ask.
  • Notes you send: to answer you and to improve the app.

We do not use any of this for advertising. We do not sell it. We do not send it to anyone to build a profile of you for money.

Keeping a copy

You can connect your own Dropbox account in Settings, and the app backs up your records there. That copy is yours and is held under Dropbox’s own terms. Prothesis cannot open it. You can also export your records as one file at any time.

Others involved

  • Apple. You get the app through Apple. When you speak a note, Apple’s speech recognition turns it into text and may process the audio on Apple’s servers. During testing, Apple’s TestFlight may send us crash reports and any feedback you choose to send through it. Apple’s own privacy policy covers what Apple collects.
  • Dropbox, only if you connect it.
  • Bible audio. When you play Bible audio, the app fetches it from a free public Bible audio service (bible.helloao.org), and from our own server if that fails. As with any download, that source sees the request. No account details are sent to it.
  • Hosting. Our sign-in server and the records above are held with Amazon Web Services, in the United States (Ohio). Encrypted backup copies of that server are kept with Backblaze, in the United States. Cloudflare runs our domain’s address book and serves our copy of the Bible audio.

We do not share your details with anyone except the companies named above that hold them for us, and where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your sign-up details and your Scripture references while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete both from our working systems within 30 days, and we email you when the request is received and again when it is done. Backup copies are overwritten within 30 days. We may keep what the law requires us to keep, and totals that identify no one.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. Before it is deleted, the app offers to export your records so you can keep them. If you no longer have the app, write to support@dovespath.app and we will delete it.

Children

Dove’s Path is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect details from a child under 13. If we learn we have, we delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we receive, how we use it or how long we keep it, we will tell you in the app and by email before the change takes effect. If we want to use what we already hold in a new way, we will ask you first.
