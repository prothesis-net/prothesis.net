About Prothesis

What it is for

What Prothesis is for is not better work, though the work changes. It is a person in whom Christ can be seen — heard, walked with, obeyed — shining His light in His people.

Christ magnified, in the ordinary places where people work & learn.

“For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD as the waters cover the sea.”

Habakkuk 2:14

Prothesis is a software company, here to steward what God has put in our hands. Our first app carries one practice: hearing God in His Word each morning & walking it out through the working day. It is the practice our founder keeps, now made for anyone to receive. You read one passage slowly, listen for what He is saying to you, & then take it with you into the hours that follow. The software serves the practice, & the practice is there to keep you near Him.

We hold that all Scripture is breathed out by God, & that He continues to speak through it.

The name

πρόθεσις

Prothesis: a purpose set forth beforehand.

It is the word Scripture uses for God’s own purpose:

“called according to His purpose” Romans 8:28

“the purpose of Him who works all things according to the counsel of His will” Ephesians 1:11

It is also the word for the temple bread of the Presence, the bread set forth continually before the Lord.

Who this is for

His purpose for you was set forth before you went looking for it. Like that bread, it is set apart & kept before Him, for you. You do not invent it or earn it. It is called for you to receive.

That is as true at a kitchen table before the house wakes as it is on a night shift, in a loud classroom, or at a desk with too much on it. You do not need a different life to hear Him, because He speaks into the one you already have. Whatever you do for a living, & whatever this morning holds, He has set something before you, & it is for you.
